# demo-classifier

Classify a short text snippet as either "positive" or "negative" sentiment.

Input: a plain-text sentence (one line).
Output: exactly one word — either `positive` or `negative`.

Sample instances:
- "The service was excellent and staff were very helpful." -> positive
- "I had a terrible experience and would not recommend." -> negative
- "The food arrived on time and tasted great." -> positive
